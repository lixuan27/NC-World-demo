RevealTable: an object permanence task for streaming video world models

Two rows of five slots hold five distinguishable goods. The top row keeps its lids open and stays visible, which makes it a same frame control on rendering ability. The bottom row keeps its lids closed, so a swap moves lids alone and the goods underneath have to be remembered. One action fills one chunk and therefore one causal commit, and an uncover action lifts the lids of the bottom row for four of the eight frames of that chunk. This page carries the task, the rollouts of our models, and the baselines they are measured against.

the task

Ground truth episodes

Six variants cover the four axes the experiments sweep, namely the algebra of the update, the density of the uncover supervision, a visible state indicator, and events that the action does not announce. Every episode is a deterministic function of its seed.

our model

NC-World rollouts at thirty thousand steps

Every clip in this section comes from the same NC-World recipe on one 0.12B block causal backbone, namely rectified flow with an independent noise level per chunk, committed history forcing, schedule aligned noise sampling and a rolling cache with one permanent anchor. Arms differ in the uncover schedule they trained on and in the cache window they run with, and nothing else. Ground truth is on the left of each panel and the streaming rollout is on the right, produced chunk by chunk from the opening chunk and the action sequence alone.

baselines and failure cases

What the same task looks like when memory or supervision is missing

Two comparisons sit beside the rollouts above. The frozen frame baseline is the floor every table in the paper carries, and it repeats the last context frame for the rest of the episode. The sparse supervision arm is our own negative result, where one uncover action in nine leaves that action unlearned, so the lids stay down and the covered row is undecodable.

Rendering: canvas 320×240, playback 15 frames per second, task clips shown at twice their native size and paired panels at one and a half times. Readings: exact match scores the whole covered permutation against chance of 0.008, per slot scores each of the five slots against chance of 0.200, and undecodable counts frames where the decoder declines to commit, which separates a failure to render from a failure to remember. Passing ground truth video through the same autoencoder and decoding the result returns exact match 1.000 on all six variants, so the codec bounds none of these readings.
